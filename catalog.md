# Catalog

Index of every document in this repository. Each entry gives the citation, where the file is physically stored, and (where relevant) which *other* topics it also speaks to — so a case is never duplicated across folders. If you're looking for material on a topic, search this file for that topic name in the "Also relevant to" column, not just the folder name.

**Convention:** a document is filed once, in its *primary* subject folder. Cross-topic relevance is recorded here, not by copying the file.

## Criminal-Procedure/Arrest-and-PMLA-Safeguards
Constitutional safeguards on arrest (Art. 21/22), grounds-of-arrest requirements under PMLA/UAPA/CrPC-BNSS, and consequences of non-compliance. This cluster of cases builds on and overrules each other in sequence — read chronologically.

| Case | Citation | Folder location | Holds | Also relevant to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pankaj Bansal v. Union of India | (2024) 7 SCC 576 : 2023 SCC OnLine SC 1244 | `Arrest-and-PMLA-Safeguards/Pankaj Bansal v Union of India.pdf` | S.19(3) PMLA — a remand order does not by itself validate an otherwise illegal arrest; court must verify S.19 PMLA conditions were satisfied before remanding; **held prospective** in Ram Kishor Arora v. ED, (2024) 7 SCC 599 | Fundamental Rights (Art. 22(1)), Remand |
| Prabir Purkayastha v. State (NCT of Delhi) | (2024) 8 SCC 254 : 2024 SCC OnLine SC 934 | `Arrest-and-PMLA-Safeguards/Prabir Purkayastha v State (NCT of Delhi).pdf` | Right to be "informed" of grounds of arrest under Art. 22(1)/S.19(1) PMLA/S.43-B(1) UAPA equated with right to be "communicated" full particulars under S.50(1) CrPC; detention illegal if grounds not provided as required for arrest; overrules *Moin Akhtar Qureshi v. UoI* and *Chagan Chandrakant Bhujbal v. UoI* | Preventive Detention (Art. 22(5)), Fundamental Rights, UAPA |
| Vihaan Kumar v. State of Haryana | (2025) 5 SCC 799 : 2025 SCC OnLine SC 269 | `Arrest-and-PMLA-Safeguards/Vihaan Kumar v State of Haryana.pdf` | Written grounds of arrest must be furnished to the arrested person (per curiam); "communicate" under Art. 22(1)/(5) requires sufficient knowledge of the basic facts imparted effectively, in writing, in a language the arrestee understands | Fundamental Rights (Art. 21), Vitiation of arrest |
| Mihir Rajesh Shah v. State of Maharashtra | (2026) 1 SCC 500 : 2025 SCC OnLine SC 2356 | `Arrest-and-PMLA-Safeguards/Mihir Rajesh Shah v State of Maharashtra.pdf` | Furnishing grounds of arrest under Art. 22(1) r/w Art. 21 held mandatory in **every** case, including ordinary IPC/BNS offences (not just PMLA/UAPA); effectuated by Ss. 50/47 CrPC/BNSS; non-compliance renders arrest illegal; overrules *Moin Akhtar Qureshi* and *Chagan Chandrakant Bhujbal* | Fundamental Rights, general (non-special-statute) arrests |
| Jaskaran Jeet Singh Deol v. State of Punjab | 2026 INSC 1022 (Criminal Appeal No. 4234 of 2026) | `Arrest-and-PMLA-Safeguards/Jaskaran Jeet Singh Deol v State of Punjab.pdf` | Whether an accused who suffered breach of Art. 22(1) and (2) can be **re-arrested** for the same offence — process for rearrest laid down where original arrest is vitiated for non-compliance; compensation as a public law remedy for Art. 22(2) violation | Fundamental Rights, Public law remedies/compensation |
| V. Senthil Balaji v. State (Dy. Director, ED) | (2024) 3 SCC 51 : 2023 SCC OnLine SC 934 | `Arrest-and-PMLA-Safeguards/V. Senthil Balaji v State (Deputy Director, ED).pdf` | Art. 226 writ of habeas corpus against a judicial remand order — not ordinarily maintainable, except where detention is illegal for non-compliance with mandatory requirements (e.g. S.167 CrPC/S.19 PMLA totally ignored); distinguished from a remand order with merely wrong/inadequate reasons (statutory remedy only) | PMLA, Habeas corpus, Remand |

**Note:** *Vijay Madanlal Choudhary v. Union of India*, (2023) 12 SCC 1 — the foundational PMLA ruling these cases build on (S.19 PMLA validity, applicability of CrPC/BNSS to PMLA) — is referenced throughout this cluster but the file itself is not yet in the repository.
